<h3>📅Project Development Period</h3>
July 2, 2024 – August 23, 2024


<h3>📝Project Introduction</h3>
Inspired by the successful delivery service “Baedal Minjok”. This food delivery app is designed to help users quickly and conveniently order their favorite meals. It features dedicated apps and web services for customers, store owners, and riders, ensuring a convenient and efficient experience for all users.


<h3>💭Project Objective</h3>
The goal of this project was to apply the knowledge and skills acquired through my studies to real-world scenarios. Drawing inspiration from successful platforms like Baedal Minjok, I aimed to integrate various learnings from programming and user experience (UX) design into a practical project that addresses real-world problems.

This project targets customers who want to order food easily and quickly, store owners who want to manage their order flows efficiently, and riders who seek to optimize their delivery routes and earnings. By understanding and optimizing data flows for each user, we aimed to enhance user experience and improve overall system efficiency.

<h3>🥘App Description</h3>
<h4>📞Customer App</h4>
The customer app provides a user-friendly interface to easily explore menus from various restaurants by category and order with just a few taps. It also allows users to track their order status and delivery progress in real time.

<h4>🧑‍🍳Store Web</h4>
The store web interface enables quick verification of new orders through real-time notifications and provides an overview of order history for easy management. It includes features for editing store information and menus, setting operating hours, and analyzing daily and monthly sales.

<h4>🛵Rider App</h4>
The rider app allows riders to receive new delivery requests in real time and easily check their delivery history. It also features a revenue management function, enabling riders to efficiently manage and track their daily earnings.

<h3>📌Reflections</h3>

Initially challenging, the introduction of new technologies like GraphQL provided me with confidence to effectively utilize unfamiliar technologies in future projects. Additionally, I significantly improved my database design and query writing skills through the experience of various table designs. While implementing MSA architecture and deploying to the cloud presented initial challenges, collaborating closely with team members and continuously refining the architecture helped us overcome deployment issues, gaining practical experience in cloud operations and management. This collaborative process emphasized the importance of communication in development.


<h4>Buja Kim</h4>

My prior experience as a customer and during part-time work helped me understand the service deeply, making the planning and analysis for Waguwagu enjoyable. While enhancing existing code written by other team members, I encountered instances where problems didn’t resolve as expected, impacting timelines. This experience highlighted the importance of time management and understanding others' code without changing requests and responses. Additionally, I was proud to be the first to attempt deployment within the team, checking for potential errors in the process and sharing insights with team members. Working with external APIs and WebSockets allowed us to innovate while focusing on collaboration.

<h4>Seongmin So</h4>
As we initiated the project, we considered various factors such as server selection and whether to use RDB or NoSQL databases. We debated whether server communication should happen through the frontend or utilize Kafka or Feign. I learned that a developer’s job involves determining the best solutions from multiple options, necessitating a deeper understanding of the features we used. This experience underscored the need for in-depth study about why we choose certain functions.


<h4>Heeseo Jung</h4>

It emphasized the significance of considering user perspectives and the necessity of integrating user needs and feedback into our app. Understanding the importance of UI/UX design and the need for iterative improvements based on user feedback became clear through my experiences in both frontend and backend development.
